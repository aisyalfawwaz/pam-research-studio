# Actual jbubble 64-channel fixture

Generated by `uv run python scripts/make-jbubble-reference.py` with engine 0.3.0 and unmodified jbubble 0.2.0/JAX 0.8.2 CPU. Lipid-coated 2 µm equilibrium-radius bubble, 1 MHz Hann sine drive, 100 kPa, 4 µs drive, 8 ringdown cycles, viscosity 1 mPa·s, ambient 101325 Pa, liquid density 1000 kg/m³, sound speed 1500 m/s. Adaptive Dopri5 rtol=1e-8, atol=1e-10. Source at (0,30) mm; drive centre 8 µs. RF: 64 receivers, 0.3 mm pitch, 4096 samples/20 MHz, 30 dB SNR/seed 42. Gate 4–18 µs, stride 1; five 128² maps.

Real dynamics: Rmax/R0 ≈1.20683; Rmin/R0 ≈0.748865; peak wall Mach ≈0.00373526; maximum predicted emission at 1 mm ≈219.724 Pa. ODE converged. Trajectories are model predictions, not calibrated measurements. RF uses the documented dimensionless retarded monopole bridge.

This fixture exposes limitations: dominant global peaks are displaced roughly 1.8–4.8 mm and background/peak ratios approach 1. The nonlinear emission has harmonics above the aperture's spatial Nyquist; anti-alias filtering removes about 0.432% of full emission energy above acquisition Nyquist. Narrower FWHM does not imply correct localization. The dataset demonstrates functioning calculation/exchange, not universally accurate PAM or a validated experiment. Full measurements and warnings are in BENCHMARK.json and the package.
