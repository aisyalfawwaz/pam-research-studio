Evgenia Blagosklonova, Daria Dolotova, Natalia Polunina, Elena Grigorieva, Denis Pakhomov, Vladimir Krylov, Andrey Gavrilov. Open dataset of annotated DICOM images of head CTA for intracranial aneurysm detection (2025). DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15697196.

One axial slice selected. Pixel samples and HU rescale/spacing retained exactly; decompressed to explicit little endian. Metadata rebuilt with public-reference identifiers; patient/study/private fields are not copied. ImagePosition reset to local zero; no patient 3D registration. No archive-wide MD5 verification is claimed for this partial download.

This is an anatomical research input, not a calibrated skull acoustic measurement or clinical demonstration. Automated tests use synthetic DICOM only.
